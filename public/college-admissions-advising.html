College & Admissions Advising

A plan that starts with you.

Make your college search more manageable with guidance on your priorities, your options and your next step. Bring your questions; you do not need a finished plan.

Explore this guide ↓

What we can work through together.

01

Your priorities

Talk through what you want from college: subjects, support, community, location and the cost your family can manage.

02

Your college list

Compare academic and financial fit across a range of options. A balanced list includes colleges you would be glad to attend, not just names you recognize.

03

Your application plan

Break requirements into milestones, identify questions for your counselor and make space for drafts, recommendations and final reviews.

Build a balanced list.

  1. Begin with non-negotiables

    Write down your must-haves and practical limits. Separate them from preferences you could trade off.

  2. Research beyond the headline

    Look at course catalogs, student support and graduation outcomes. Use each college’s net price calculator to explore affordability.

  3. Keep options open

    Consider a range of admissions selectivity, including realistic and affordable options. No admission or aid outcome is guaranteed.

  4. Review your list again

    As you learn more, remove colleges that no longer fit and record why the remaining ones matter to you.
