Long-Term Mentorship

Keep looking forward.

Your goals can change as you learn more about yourself. Mentorship offers space to reflect, plan and keep moving beyond a single application deadline.

Explore this guide ↓

A conversation that can grow with you.

01

Explore

Name your interests and uncertainties. Choose something small to try, such as researching a course or talking with someone in a field that interests you.

02

Act

Choose a next step that fits your time and circumstances. Make the task clear enough to know when you have done it.

03

Reflect

Discuss what you learned, what felt difficult and what you would change. Adjust your plan as your priorities develop.

A simple check-in agenda.

  1. Since last time

    What did I try? What surprised me?

  2. Right now

    What is one obstacle I want to work through?

  3. Before next time

    What will I do, what support do I need and when will I revisit it?

Start with your situation.

Tell us what you hope to work on and what kind of guidance would help. Discuss expectations and availability with us rather than assuming a particular meeting schedule or assigned mentor.
