College Planning

A little clarity goes a long way.

Build a flexible plan around your next decision. These phases are a starting point, not a fixed calendar; use official deadlines for your colleges and entry term.

Explore this guide ↓

A timeline you can adapt.

  1. Explore your options

    Reflect on interests, academic preparation and support needs. Research several types of colleges and talk about affordability early.

  2. Build your list

    Compare programs and estimated costs. Record application routes, required materials and deadlines in one place.

  3. Prepare your applications

    Allow time for writing and feedback. Ask recommenders early, request school documents and confirm each college’s instructions.

  4. Review decisions

    Compare admission conditions and complete aid offers, not just award headlines. Ask questions before making a commitment.

  5. Prepare for the transition

    Track enrollment tasks, housing, orientation and final documents. Locate academic and wellbeing support before you need it.
