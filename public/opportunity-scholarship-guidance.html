Opportunity & Scholarship Guidance

Make room for opportunity.

Explore scholarships and learning opportunities with a plan for checking eligibility, deadlines and fit. A focused search can be more useful than a long list of links.

Explore this guide ↓

Build a search you can maintain.

  1. Start close to home

    Ask your school counselor about local foundations, community organizations and school-based opportunities. Check colleges’ own scholarship pages.

  2. Read the requirements first

    Review applicant status, location, course of study and any renewal conditions before spending time on an application.

  3. Track the complete commitment

    Record the official link, due date, materials and whether an award is renewable. For programs, include travel, attendance and participation costs.

  4. Choose where to focus

    Prioritize opportunities that fit your situation and leave time for required essays or recommendations.

Questions worth asking.

01

What will it cover?

Check how funds are paid and what expenses they may support. Ask the college how an outside award could affect your financial-aid offer.

02

What happens next year?

Look for renewal conditions, continuing eligibility and any separate annual application.

03

Is the source trustworthy?

Confirm the sponsor and official website. Be cautious about guaranteed awards, pressure to act or requests for payment to access aid.
