Essay & Storytelling Support

Your story. Your voice.

You do not need a dramatic life event to write meaningfully. Begin with a specific moment, what you did and what it shows about how you think.

Explore this guide ↓

Support at each stage.

01

Find a starting point

Collect moments from school, work, family responsibilities, hobbies or your community. Notice where you made a choice, solved a problem or changed your mind.

02

Shape a draft

Choose one idea to explore. Use concrete details, then make room to explain why the experience matters to you now.

03

Revise with purpose

Ask whether the draft answers the prompt, sounds like you and adds something useful to the application. Revise structure before polishing individual sentences.

Student-led, always.

We can help you brainstorm, organize ideas and consider feedback. Your application writing must remain your own work. Do not invent experiences or ask someone else to write your submission. Follow each application’s authorship and AI-use rules.

A useful feedback conversation.

  1. Tell your reader what you need

    Ask about one concern: clarity, structure or whether your main idea comes through.

  2. Listen for patterns

    If readers are confused in the same place, revisit that section. You do not have to accept every stylistic suggestion.

  3. Read it aloud

    Notice language you would never use in conversation. Keep the meaning and make the voice your own.
