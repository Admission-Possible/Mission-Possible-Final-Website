Application Guidance

One step at a time.

Applications feel more manageable when each requirement has a place. Start with your college’s official instructions, then build a checklist around your applicant type and entry term.

Explore this guide ↓

From requirements to submission.

  1. Choose the right application

    Check the college’s admissions website for accepted platforms and first-year or transfer instructions. A shared platform does not mean shared deadlines.

  2. Gather your materials

    Organize coursework, activities and responsibilities. Ask your school how to request transcripts and recommendations; allow time for other people’s work.

  3. Plan the writing

    List each required essay, supplement or portfolio. Keep prompts with your drafts so you can check that every response answers the question.

  4. Review and follow through

    Preview each submission, confirm any fee or waiver steps and save the confirmation. Check the college portal afterward for missing documents.

Before you press submit.
