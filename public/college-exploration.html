College Exploration

Find your kind of place.

A college is more than its ranking. Explore how you want to learn, where you want to live and what helps you feel supported.

Explore this guide ↓

Look through three lenses.

01

Academic life

Compare majors, sample courses, class sizes and flexibility to change direction. Ask how students access research, advising or hands-on learning.

02

Daily life

Consider campus size, location, housing, transport and communities you might join. Imagine an ordinary weekday, not just a campus tour.

03

Practical fit

Compare estimated net costs, travel expenses, accessibility and available support. Check whether important resources have eligibility requirements.

Ask better questions on a visit.

In the classroom
What happens when a student struggles in an introductory course? How easy is it to meet an instructor?
Outside the classroom
Where do students spend time between classes? What is campus like on weekends?
Before applying
Can I study my intended subject here? What is the estimated cost for my situation, and how might it change?

Try a two-college comparison.

Choose two colleges that interest you. For each, record one course you would take, one support resource you might use, an estimated net cost and one unanswered question. Notice what matters most when you compare them.
